Foundation

Playful, expansive,felt, not explained.

Clay's brand team describes the brand as "playful, expansive, creative" and says "the brand is felt, not explained." The visual metaphor is literal clay: stop-motion plasticine machines moving little colored balls — data — through pipelines. The stance is explicitly against the "sea of grey websites, AI-generated blurry textures." Craft is the differentiator. CONFIRMED — Clay's own posts & JDs

Positioning: Clay invented GTM engineering. The product is infrastructure — "Build systems to grow revenue." Play lives in the visuals; the copy stays composed.

The stacked-card scroll CONFIRMED — pure CSS

Each feature card is position: sticky; top: 72px — every new card slides up over the last. No JavaScript, no scrub. The one motion moment the page is remembered by.

The house easing CONFIRMED ×21

cubic-bezier(0.075, 0.82, 0.165, 1) at 0.3s — fast start, soft landing. Button hovers change background color only: black → grey-900 #282C35. No lifts, no shadows.

The supporting cast

Marquee — logo strip loops via translateX(-50%) keyframe.

bouncy — rotateZ 0→−4°→0 wobble on playful props.

fadein — opacity-only entrances, used sparingly.

What Clay doesn't do: scroll-jacking, parallax, elevation. GSAP is loaded, but the homepage runs zero ScrollTriggers. CONFIRMED live

Components · Clay Brand Book09
Voice · full guide in clay-tone-of-voice.md

A confident engineer,talking to a builder.

Builder-confident

direct · never arrogant or salesy

Verb-first, second person, contractions. Vocabulary: build, systems, plays, GTM, orchestrate, reps, signals.

"Build systems to grow revenue"

Absurdly specific

numbers + mechanism · never vague

Proof = bold company + multiplier verb + metric + how. No number? Cut the claim. The villain is manual work, never a named competitor.

"Mistral AI cut TAM mapping from 2 months to 10 days."

Quietly playful

one wink per page · never jokey, no emoji, no "!"

The play lives in the visuals; copy stays composed. Max one rhetorical-question headline.

"Why use one contact database when you can use them all?"

Tone dial for the prospect pages: homepage confidence + pricing-page warmth — the reader already booked the demo. Prime, don't re-sell. Banned: revolutionary, cutting-edge, best-in-class, game-changing, "leverage," exclamation marks.
